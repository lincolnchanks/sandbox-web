Human

For millennia, humans have been regarded as masters of their home realm, the Overworld. Carrying with them unique stories, cultures, and abilities, humans have blazed a trail of exploration and progress, one which eventually led to their domination of the Overworld.

Ancient legends whisper of a time when humans and monsters worked together, setting aside their differences to unite against a common threat. Those times, if they existed at all, are long gone now.

Villager

Humans may have conquered the Overworld, but Villagers founded it. According to legend, this race was created by the Hosts long before the arrival of humans in the Overworld.

Villagers have always been a peaceful group, but not all of them are satisfied with their settled-down lifestyle. Some have turned to scholarship, exploration, and even fighting. Some Villagers have even founded nations.

Illager

Deep in the forests and across the Overworld, a darker presence watches in silence. Illagers, the cousin race to the Villagers, are infamous among residents of the Overworld. While they keep to their own, they're not above pillaging to get what they want. Many of them have tapped into dark magic as well.

Enderman

Humans rule the Overworld, and Piglins and Wither Skeletons fight over the Nether. The Overworld and Nether, in turn, are constantly battling one another.

But the Endermen? They are silent. Aloof. Separated from the conflicts of the Overworld and Nether, they build their kingdoms in the End.
