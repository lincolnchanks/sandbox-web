Human

For millennia, humans have been regarded as masters of their home realm, the Overworld. Carrying with them unique stories, cultures, and abilities, humans have blazed a trail of exploration and progress, one which eventually led to their domination of the Overworld.

Ancient legends whisper of a time when humans and monsters worked together, setting aside their differences to unite against a common threat. Those times, if they existed at all, are long gone now.
