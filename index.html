Human

For as long as the clock has counted, the human race has stood the test of time as masters of their home realm, the Overworld. This race, which bears countless stories, cultures, and abilities, has blazed a trail of exploration and progress that continues to forever change the Overworld as we know it.

Older tales whisper of a time when humans worked together with those who now serve them. A time when the Overworld was threatened, and all living creatures therein set aside their differences to vanquish the threat. Those times, if they even existed in the first place, are all but legend now.

Villager

Humans may have conquered the Overworld, but Villagers founded it. According to legend, this race was created by the Hosts long before the arrival of humans in the Overworld.

Villagers have always been a peaceful group, but not all of them are satisfied with their settled-down lifestyle. Some have turned to scholarship, exploration, and even combat. Some Villagers have even founded nations.

Illager

Deep in the forests and across the Overworld, a darker presence watches in silence. Illagers, the cousin race to the Villagers, are infamous among residents of the Overworld. This group is known for pillaging, terrorism, and other heinous acts. Most illagers are common foot soldiers, but several of them take to darker forms of magic, tapping into dangerous wells of power to further their own agendas.

Piglin

Humans avoid the Nether for a reason. It's hot. It's dark. It's dangerous. Piglins are just fine with that, so long as they are left alone. Once a mighty empire, the Piglin race is now a shell of its former glory, reduced to nothing but wandering tribes.

Awakened Undead

You died. A long time ago. Depending on the circumstances, you may remember your past life. You may not. No two Awakened have the same experience. What you do know is that you weren't always this way. Between the time that you died and the time you were Awakened, you were a shell. A mindless slave to your own appetites.

All of that changed when a Necromancer brought you back. Not from death, for no soul can be fully claimed from its dark prison. Rather, you were merely freed from the bonds and limitations of your undead state. Given consciousness, strength, and awareness you haven't possessed in a long time.

You aren't the same person you were in your former life. You may never be or know that person ever again. But you are here, now, and that is all that matters right now.

Enderman

Humans rule the Overworld, and Piglins and Wither Skeletons fight over the Nether. The Overworld and Nether, in turn, are constantly battling one another.

But the Endermen? They are silent. Aloof. Separated from the conflicts of the Overworld and Nether, they build their kingdoms in the End.

Description

The Minecraft RPG is a tabletop roleplaying game set in the world of Minecraft. It is a medium for inventing stories, worlds, and characters within the most popular sandbox game of all time, Minecraft.

The purpose of the Minecraft RPG is not to provide an objective story to the world of Minecraft. Its purpose is to provide the groundwork for players and Game Masters to tell their own unique stories within the world.

That said, the core set will probably have a lot of pre-baked worlds.
